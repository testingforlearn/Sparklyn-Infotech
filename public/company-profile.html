Our Project Portfolio

Sparklyn Infotech has engineered purposeful software applications spanning assistive mobile AI, enterprise retail management, and online educational test platforms. Below are highlights of our flagship projects:

PROJECT 01: SIGNOVA – SMART SIGN LANGUAGE ASSISTANT MOBILE APP
Mobile App & Assistive AI

Overview: Signova is an innovative, assistive mobile application specially developed for individuals who do not know sign language. It empowers non-signers to seamlessly communicate and bridge conversations with the deaf and hard-of-hearing community in classrooms, workplaces, healthcare clinics, and daily public interactions.

Key Capabilities & Features:
  • Two-Way Speech-to-Sign Translation: Captures spoken speech or typed text and converts it into animated sign language demonstrations in real time.
  • Real-Time AI Camera Gesture Recognition: Utilizes mobile computer vision to identify sign language gestures captured by the device camera and translates them into readable text and synthesized speech.
  • Interactive Learning & Dictionary Module: Comprehensive categorized dictionary with step-by-step visual tutorials for users to practice and master basic to advanced sign vocabulary.
  • Emergency Quick Phrases & Offline Mode: Pre-loaded essential emergency phrases for instant accessibility without requiring continuous internet connectivity.
Technologies & Architecture: Flutter, Dart, TensorFlow Lite, Computer Vision, Python, Firebase Cloud Services.
PROJECT 02: INVENTORY, SALES & ACCOUNTING MANAGEMENT SYSTEM
Full-Stack Web Application

Overview: Developed a comprehensive web-based application to manage inventory, product stock, sales transactions, and accounting operations efficiently. Implemented modern features to streamline business workflows, maintain organized records, and simplify sales and inventory tracking for commercial retail and wholesale businesses.

Key Capabilities & Features:
  • Real-Time Stock & Inventory Management: Tracks stock movements across multi-product catalogs, monitors minimum threshold levels, and provides automatic low-stock notifications.
  • Fast Point-of-Sale (POS) & Billing: Generates instant GST-compliant tax invoices, receipts, and order confirmations with integrated barcode lookup and payment tracking.
  • Automated Double-Entry Accounting & Ledgers: Automates customer and vendor ledger accounts, tracks outstanding payables and receivables, and generates real-time cash flow summaries.
  • Financial Reporting & Revenue Analytics: Interactive dashboard visualizations displaying daily, monthly, and yearly sales graphs, top-selling products, and gross/net profit-and-loss margins.
Technologies & Architecture: React.js, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL Database, TailwindCSS, Chart.js Data Visualization.

PROJECT 03: GOVEXAM TEST PORTAL – ONLINE EXAMINATION PLATFORM
EdTech Web Application

Overview: A full-featured online exam testing website built specifically for government exam preparation aspirants (such as TNPSC, UPSC, SSC, Banking, and RRB). Designed to enable candidates to easily attend timed tests and view their score instantly in an authentic real-exam testing environment.

Key Capabilities & Features:
  • Authentic Computer-Based Test (CBT) Interface: Simulates official government exam UI with subject section switching, countdown timers, and full-screen distraction-free mode.
  • Interactive Question Palette: Live status indicators for answered, unanswered, marked for review, and visited questions for optimal time management.
  • Instant Automated Scorecard & Analytics: Immediate calculation of total marks, negative mark deductions, accuracy rate, percentile standing, and time spent per question upon submission.
  • Question-by-Question Solution Keys: In-depth explanatory answer keys and reference notes to help candidates identify weaknesses and improve test performance.
Technologies & Architecture: React.js, Node.js, Express.js, MongoDB Database, RESTful APIs, TailwindCSS.
